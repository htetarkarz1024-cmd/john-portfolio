What I do

01

Always-on paid media

Monthly Meta, Google and TikTok management for a venue: planning, media buying, creative rotation, weekly optimisation and a monthly report that shows bookings and spend, not just reach.

Proof: Fira Beach Club, reservations up 119% on the same six months of 2025, all channels →

02

Launches, festivals and events

Date-phased campaigns for openings, festivals and seasonal offers: awareness first, then the booking push, then a final-week countdown, with the budget moving to what sells.

Proof: World Gourmet Festival, $41,070 of web sales during a $6,610 campaign →

03

Tracking and measurement

Meta Pixel, Conversion API, Google Tag Manager and GA4 set up and checked before any money is spent, with bookings counted through TableCheck, SevenRooms, LINE or WhatsApp, so reports show what the ads brought in, not just clicks.

Proof: Iris & Wild Iris, 34 bookings tracked to Meta and Google in SevenRooms →

04

White-label for agencies

I run campaigns inside agency teams under your brand: you keep the client relationship, I do the planning, buying, tracking and reporting. I already work this way with two agencies, on venues in Thailand and Indonesia.

Proof: the case studies were delivered through agency teams →
