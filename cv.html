Profile

I'm John, a freelance paid-media specialist based in Bangkok. I have run paid campaigns since 2022, and since 2024 mainly for hospitality venues in Thailand and Indonesia: hotel restaurants and festivals in Bangkok, beach clubs in Phuket, restaurants and spas on Koh Samui.
